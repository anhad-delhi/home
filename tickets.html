Before you book

  • Tickets are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged once purchased. If the performance is cancelled or rescheduled by the organisers, you will be offered a seat at the new date or a full refund.
  • Right of admission reserved. The organisers and the venue may refuse or revoke entry at their discretion.
  • Tickets are transferable, but only one person may be admitted per ticket.
  • Carry a valid photo ID and your booking confirmation to the venue.
  • Latecomers will be seated at a suitable break in the set, at the usher's discretion.
  • Flash photography and recording during the performance are not permitted.
  • Attendees under 12 are admitted free but must be accompanied by a ticket-holding adult.

Payment is handled by Razorpay. You'll be taken there to complete the booking.
